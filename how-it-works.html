How this mortgage calculator works

This calculator estimates the monthly cost of a conventional fixed-rate mortgage. Enter the home price, down payment, annual interest rate and loan term. The calculator then estimates principal and interest and adds the monthly equivalents of property tax, homeowners insurance, PMI and HOA fees.

Monthly principal and interest formula

The principal-and-interest portion uses the standard fixed-rate amortization formula:

M = P × [r(1 + r)^n] ÷ [(1 + r)^n − 1]

M is the monthly principal-and-interest payment, P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate, and n is the total number of monthly payments.

What the calculator fields mean

Home price
The purchase price of the property before the down payment.
Down payment
The amount paid upfront. A larger down payment reduces the amount borrowed.
Interest rate
The annual mortgage interest rate used to estimate principal and interest.
Loan term
The number of years used to repay the mortgage. Common terms include 15 and 30 years.
Property tax
Your estimated annual property-tax expense. Actual rates vary by location and property.
Home insurance
Your estimated annual homeowners-insurance premium.
PMI
An estimated annual PMI rate. Actual PMI pricing and cancellation rules depend on the loan and lender.
HOA fees
Monthly homeowners-association dues, if applicable.
