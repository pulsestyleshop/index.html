About Mortgage Calculator

Welcome to Mortgage Calculator. We are dedicated to providing simple, accurate, and easy-to-use financial tools to help you make informed decisions about your home buying journey.

Our Mission

Buying a home is one of the most significant financial commitments you will ever make. Our mission is to demystify the mortgage process. We believe that everyone should have access to clear, transparent calculations without needing a finance degree.

What We Do

Our free calculator performs all calculations locally in your browser. This means your financial data remains completely private. By inputting the home price, interest rate, and loan term, you can instantly see your estimated monthly payments, including principal, interest, taxes, insurance, and PMI. We also provide a detailed amortization schedule so you can see how your loan balance decreases over time.

Educational Purposes Only

Please note that this tool is designed for educational and planning purposes. We do not provide personalized mortgage offers, loan approvals, or individualized financial advice. The estimates provided by our calculator may differ from your lender's actual numbers due to specific fees, escrow adjustments, and individual loan terms. Always consult with a qualified mortgage professional for your specific needs.
