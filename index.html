How this mortgage calculator works

This calculator estimates the monthly cost of a conventional fixed-rate mortgage. Enter the home price, down payment, annual interest rate and loan term. The calculator then estimates principal and interest and adds the monthly equivalents of property tax, homeowners insurance, PMI and HOA fees.

Monthly principal and interest formula

The principal-and-interest portion uses the standard fixed-rate amortization formula:

M = P × [r(1 + r)^n] ÷ [(1 + r)^n − 1]

M is the monthly principal-and-interest payment, P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate, and n is the total number of monthly payments.

Example

For a $400,000 home with an $80,000 down payment, the estimated loan amount is $320,000. At a 6.5% fixed annual rate over 30 years, principal and interest is approximately $2,023 per month. Taxes, insurance, PMI and HOA costs are separate and can increase the total monthly housing cost.

What the calculator fields mean

Home price
The purchase price of the property before the down payment.
Down payment
The amount paid upfront. A larger down payment reduces the amount borrowed.
Interest rate
The annual mortgage interest rate used to estimate principal and interest.
Loan term
The number of years used to repay the mortgage. Common terms include 15 and 30 years.
Property tax
Your estimated annual property-tax expense. Actual rates vary by location and property.
Home insurance
Your estimated annual homeowners-insurance premium.
PMI
An estimated annual PMI rate. Actual PMI pricing and cancellation rules depend on the loan and lender.
HOA fees
Monthly homeowners-association dues, if applicable.

Ways to compare mortgage scenarios

  • Compare different down-payment amounts to see how the loan size changes.
  • Compare 15-year and 30-year terms to understand payment and interest differences.
  • Test several interest-rate assumptions rather than relying on one quote.
  • Include taxes, insurance and HOA costs so the estimate is closer to the total housing expense.
  • Use your lender's Loan Estimate and final disclosures for actual loan costs.

Frequently asked questions

How much house can I afford?

Affordability depends on income, debts, credit, down payment, interest rate, taxes, insurance and other expenses. Use this calculator to explore scenarios, then discuss your personal situation with a qualified mortgage professional.

Does this calculator include taxes and insurance?

Yes. Enter annual property tax and homeowners insurance to add their estimated monthly amounts to the payment.

Does the calculator include PMI?

Yes. You can enter an estimated annual PMI percentage. Actual PMI may be calculated differently depending on the mortgage program, lender and borrower.

Are the results exact?

No. They are estimates. Actual payments can differ because of lender fees, escrow adjustments, rate changes, insurance, taxes, mortgage-insurance rules and other loan terms.

Why can the total monthly payment be higher than principal and interest?

Principal and interest are only part of the cost of owning a mortgaged home. Property taxes, homeowners insurance, PMI and HOA dues can add to the monthly housing expense.
