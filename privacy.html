Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This website's mortgage calculator processes the numbers you enter in your browser. The calculator itself does not require your name, email address, account or other personal information.

Information processed by the calculator

Loan values such as home price, down payment, interest rate and loan term are used by JavaScript in your browser to produce the calculation. This page does not send those calculator inputs to a server as part of the calculator function.

Advertising and cookies

If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled, advertising providers may use cookies, device information and similar technologies according to their own policies and applicable privacy requirements. Personalized advertising and consent choices can vary by visitor location and browser settings.

Third-party services

Third-party services, including advertising and analytics providers if enabled, may process information under their own privacy policies. We do not control how independent third parties process information.

Privacy choices

Visitors can manage cookies and advertising preferences through their browser and, where provided, the consent or privacy controls displayed by the relevant service.
