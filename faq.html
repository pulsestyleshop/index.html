Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our mortgage calculator and home loans.

How much house can I afford?

Affordability depends on income, debts, credit, down payment, interest rate, taxes, insurance and other expenses. Use this calculator to explore scenarios, then discuss your personal situation with a qualified mortgage professional.

Does this calculator include taxes and insurance?

Yes. Enter annual property tax and homeowners insurance to add their estimated monthly amounts to the payment.

Does the calculator include PMI?

Yes. You can enter an estimated annual PMI percentage. Actual PMI may be calculated differently depending on the mortgage program, lender and borrower.

Are the results exact?

No. They are estimates. Actual payments can differ because of lender fees, escrow adjustments, rate changes, insurance, taxes, mortgage-insurance rules and other loan terms.
